## 1. Purpose

To establish management direction and commitment for information security management, aligned with business requirements, applicable laws and regulations.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to the entire organization, including:

- All employees, contractors, and third parties with access to information assets
- All information systems, IT infrastructure, and managed data
- All business processes handling information

## 3. Policy Statement

Management of [EMPRESA] commits to:

1. **Protect information** in all forms (digital, physical, verbal) by ensuring its confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

2. **Comply with legal** and regulatory requirements, including personal data protection and contractual obligations.

3. **Manage risks** to information security through a systematic process of risk identification, assessment, and treatment.

4. **Implement controls** proportional to identified risks, based on Annex A of ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

5. **Continually improve** the Information Security Management System (ISMS).

6. **Provide resources** necessary for the implementation and maintenance of the ISMS.

## 4. Core Principles

### 4.1 Confidentiality
Information is classified and protected against unauthorized access based on its sensitivity level.

### 4.2 Integrity
Controls are implemented to ensure the accuracy and completeness of information and processing methods.

### 4.3 Availability
Information and services are available when needed, with continuity plans for disruptions.

## 5. ISMS Framework

The ISMS operates under the PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act) cycle:

- **Plan:** Risk assessment, objectives definition, and controls selection
- **Do:** Implementation of controls and procedures
- **Check:** Monitoring, measurement, internal audits
- **Act:** Corrective actions and continual improvement

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Management | Policy approval, resource allocation, ISMS review |
| Security Officer | ISMS coordination, risk management, reporting |
| IT Manager | Technical control implementation, monitoring |
| All employees | Policy compliance, incident reporting |

## 8. Non-Compliance

Non-compliance with this policy may result in disciplinary action according to internal work regulations and, in serious cases, legal liability.

## 9. Review

This policy will be reviewed at least annually or when significant changes occur in the organization, technology, or threat landscape.
